# CrazyGames submission — Ember Vigil

## Title
Ember Vigil

## Description
Monsters pour out of the dark winter forest. Keep your lantern lit and survive until dawn.

Ember Vigil is a survival roguelite where you only control your movement – your weapons fire on their own. Collect blue crystals to level up and build your own combination of rune weapons and blessings: burning bolts, a ring of embers, firepots, Tyr's spear and northern lights that chain between foes. Face three bosses during the night, from a fell troll to the Frost Giant.

Every game earns embers, even when you fall. Spend them on permanent upgrades and new heroes, then go back out and last a little longer. Survive the night to unlock the next one – ten nights, each harder and more rewarding than the last.

## Features
- Easy to pick up: move, and your weapons do the rest
- 6 rune weapons and 8 blessings to combine in every run
- 3 bosses and 5 kinds of monsters
- Permanent upgrades and 3 heroes to unlock
- 10 nights of rising difficulty
- Plays on desktop and mobile

## Controls
- Move: WASD or arrow keys (desktop), drag anywhere (mobile)
- Pick an upgrade: click, tap or press 1–4
- Pause: Esc or P
- Mute: M
