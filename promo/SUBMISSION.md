# CrazyGames submission — Ember Vigil

## Title
Ember Vigil

## Description
Monsters pour out of the dark winter forest. Keep your lantern lit and survive until dawn.

Ember Vigil is a survival roguelite where you only control your movement – your weapons fire on their own. Collect blue crystals to level up and build your own combination of rune weapons and blessings: burning bolts, a ring of embers, firepots, Tyr's spear and northern lights that chain between foes. Max out a weapon, pair it with the right blessing and open a treasure chest to evolve it into something far more powerful. Face three bosses during the night, from a fell troll to the Frost Giant.

Every game earns embers, even when you fall. Spend them on permanent upgrades and new heroes, complete goals for extra rewards, then go back out and last a little longer. Each night takes 7 minutes. Survive it to unlock the next one – ten nights, each harder and more rewarding than the last.

## Features
- Easy to pick up: move, and your weapons do the rest
- 6 rune weapons, 8 blessings and 6 weapon evolutions
- 3 bosses and 5 kinds of monsters
- 18 goals, permanent upgrades and 4 heroes to unlock
- 10 nights of rising difficulty, 7 minutes each
- Plays on desktop and mobile

## Controls
- Move: WASD or arrow keys (desktop), drag anywhere (mobile)
- Pick an upgrade: click, tap or press 1–4
- Pause: P or the pause button
- Mute: M
